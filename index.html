Signal Architecture
What goes into Flo Biological Age
Core signals — from Flo data
Available on day one, no wearable needed
🔄
Cycle length consistency
Variability in cycle length across 6–12 months. High variability is an established predictor of early cardiovascular risk and reproductive aging onset.
Flo core tracking
📅
Luteal phase length trend
Shortening luteal phases signal declining progesterone — an early marker of perimenopause transition, often appearing years before cycle irregularity.
Flo prediction model
🩸
Period duration & flow changes
Longitudinal shift in bleed length and reported flow volume. Patterns of change (not snapshots) carry the aging signal.
Flo core tracking
🌡️
PMS symptom severity trend
Worsening or improving PMS burden over time. Escalating symptoms in the luteal phase can reflect hormonal imbalance progression.
Flo symptom log
🧠
Mood & cognitive patterns
Mood consistency and cognitive complaints across phases. Research links estrogen fluctuation severity to neurological aging markers.
Flo daily log
🌊
Perimenopause transition indicators
Hot flash logs, night sweats, vaginal dryness, cycle skips. These mark progression through reproductive aging stages — a clinically validated framework.
Flo symptom log + Perimenopause mode

Engagement Design
The mechanics that make it sticky
01
Slow anchor + fast pulse
Flo Biological Age updates regularly.
"Your Flo Biological Age improved by 1 year this quarter — your sleep consistency was the biggest driver."
02
Named pillars, not black boxes
Each Flo Biological Age is built from relevant pillars. Users can see exactly what to improve — the key insight from Whoop's success.
"Your Symptom Burden pillar is pulling your score up. Your luteal phase mood symptoms have increased over 3 months."
03
Life stage framing
Unlike generic biological age, Flo Biological Age is explicitly contextualised by reproductive stage. A 38-year-old's score is interpreted differently from a 28-year-old's — making it personally meaningful rather than alarming.
"For your reproductive stage, this is excellent. You're in the top 20% of users your age."
04
Minimum data threshold
Unlocks after 3 full tracked cycles (mirrors Whoop's 21-recovery gate). Creates a sense of earning access and ensures data quality. Drives short-term logging behaviour to unlock the feature.
"Track 2 more cycles to unlock your Flo Biological Age."
05
The shareable moment
Oura built CVA into its Year in Review; Whoop users share their WHOOP Age like a fitness PB. Flo Biological Age needs a shareable card — "My body is 4 years younger" — that travels in women's health communities organically.
Annual Flo Health Report: personalised, beautiful, shareable.
06
Subscription gate (Flo Premium)
Full Flo Biological Age score + trend history behind Premium. Free users see a teaser ("Your cycle consistency is in the healthy range — see your full Flo Biological Age with Premium"). Proven conversion mechanic for health insight features.
Drives Premium conversion through aspiration, not utility alone.

Considerations
What to get right
⚠️
Medical claims boundary
Whoop got an FDA warning letter for positioning health features as diagnostic. Flo Biological Age must be framed as a wellness signal, not a clinical measure. "Wellbeing estimate" language, not "biological age diagnosis."
😟
Anxiety risk for vulnerable users
A score that says "your body is 8 years older than your age" could distress users with PCOS, endo, or irregular cycles. Needs careful framing, contextualisation by condition, and clear "this doesn't mean X" guardrails.
📉
Sparse data for irregular trackers
Users who track inconsistently — common in the 18–25 cohort — will have noisy or unavailable scores. Need a graceful degraded state, not a blank screen. Partial scores or directional signals can still engage.
🔬
Scientific validation requirement
Flo has published peer-reviewed research on cycle patterns (the 2024 medrxiv paper). Flo Biological Age will need similar validation — ideally a partnership with a reproductive aging researcher — to defend the methodology publicly.
